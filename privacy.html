Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Elemii is developed by Chen Le Yong. This policy explains the current iPhone app, this website and our limited development tests for report delivery.

1. Charts and saved information

The app calculates charts on your device. Saved names, birth details, calculated results, My chart selection and article bookmarks are stored locally. These saved records are excluded from normal backups and are not synced by Elemii to other devices. Your language choice is stored separately as an app preference.

Ordinary chart calculation does not automatically send your saved names, birth details or results to our report service or to an AI provider. There is no customer account or cloud chart library.

2. Online articles and optional city search

Elemii requests public articles and images hosted through Cloudflare. Those requests do not contain your saved names, birth details or results. Cloudflare receives normal connection information, such as your IP address, to serve the content.

For optional true solar time, Apple handles city search and timezone lookup. Those lookups use search text and selected coordinates without your name, birth date or birth time. Elemii does not request access to your current location.

3. Visiting this website

This is a static information website. It has no account sign-in, payment form, chart submission form, advertising or analytics scripts added by Elemii. It does not set cookies or use browser storage for those purposes.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub receives connection information, including your IP address, and logs it for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Google also receives information when you visit its linked services. Their policies govern their own processing.

4. Contacting support

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you provide. We use them to answer your question and investigate the issue. The app does not automatically attach birth details, charts, screenshots or logs.

Support messages are kept in the developer’s email account while needed to handle the request and related follow-up. You can ask us to delete your correspondence; separate copies may remain in your own mailbox or with email providers under their policies.

5. Report and email-delivery tests

Personal reading PDFs and email delivery are in development. Current tests use synthetic chart information, approved sample reports and recipients who agreed to the test. This website does not accept report orders or collect birth information for generation.

Separate writing tests send a prepared synthetic chart brief to OpenAI. Email addresses and Gmail authorization credentials are not part of that writing input. PDF rendering is tested through Cloudflare’s managed browser service.

For email tests, Cloudflare processes an approved recipient address, message and sample PDF, then sends them to Google’s Gmail API. Google delivers the email and may retain it in the sender’s Sent mailbox and the recipient’s mailbox. Recipients do not authorize Gmail access or share their Google credentials with Elemii.

The developer authorizes a sender Gmail account through the service named Elemii Email Test, requesting only permission to send email. We do not request permission to read inbox messages, contacts or other mailbox content. Sender authorization credentials are kept in private development storage and Cloudflare secrets, not in this website or public source files.

Google API data is used to provide the requested email delivery, not for advertising, sale, profiling or training general-purpose AI models. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention, removal and your choices

You can remove saved charts and article bookmarks in Saved. Deleting Elemii removes its local saved records, with no recovery through Elemii. Offloading the app may retain its data.

Private test reports, delivery receipts and authorization records are retained during development for review and troubleshooting. There is currently no automatic deletion schedule for these development records. Test participants can contact us to request removal. The sender can revoke access through Google Account connections; credentials must also be removed from our configured storage when the integration is retired.

Copies already delivered to another mailbox are controlled by that mailbox owner and its email provider. Deleting a development file does not recall a delivered email. Access to these records is limited to the developer and the providers involved in the test.

7. Future features and policy changes

Before customer report purchases or real birth-data transmission are enabled, we will update the privacy information and explain the information sent, providers involved, purpose, retention and delivery choices before the user proceeds. Current test results do not mean that customer report delivery is available.

We will update the date above when this policy changes. Contact chenle.yong16@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.
