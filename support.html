Support

Questions about Elemii, something that does not look right, or a privacy request? Contact the developer, Chen Le Yong.

Email support

chenle.yong16@gmail.com

This link opens your email app. Nothing is sent until you choose to send your message.

What to include

A short description of the issue, your iPhone model, iOS version and Elemii app version will help. If you attach a screenshot, remove any personal information you do not want to share.

You do not need to include your full birth details, saved charts or any passwords to ask for help. Elemii does not attach those details automatically.

Questions about a test email?

We are currently testing PDF report delivery with people who agreed to receive a sample. If a sample email is unexpected or you want its information removed from our test records, contact the address above.

No report purchases are accepted on this website. We do not promise an immediate response or a fixed support turnaround time.

Managing saved data

You can remove saved charts and article bookmarks in the app’s Saved tab. Deleting the app removes its local saved data; offloading the app is different from deleting it. Elemii does not provide an account-based recovery service.

See the privacy policy for details about storage, online articles and email tests.
