A guide for reflection

BaZi is a traditional framework, not a scientifically validated personality test. Use its themes as prompts to think about your experiences, rather than as fixed descriptions of who you are or predictions about your future.

Optional in-app purchase

Personal reading PDFs

Go deeper with a personal reading based on your chart, purchased inside Elemii through Apple. Version 1.1 offers new PDF reports in English or Simplified Chinese, following your selected app language. Each purchase provides one report in the language shown before checkout.

Open your completed report through Saved → Charts, then save or share the PDF. Online downloads remain available for 30 days after completion. This website does not accept purchases or birth details for report generation.
